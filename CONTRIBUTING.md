# Contributing

Pull requests are welcome in every Orthrus Research repository. A repository's own
`CONTRIBUTING.md` takes precedence over this page and names its build and checks.

## Before you start

- For a small fix, open the pull request directly.
- For a larger change, open an issue first, or ask on
  [Discord](https://discord.gg/qCVqhXtSzR), so the approach can be agreed before the work.
- Follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## A good pull request

- Makes one change, and says what changed and why.
- States what was run to verify it and what that showed, for example "ci passed; golden digests
  unchanged" or "new test fails before the fix, passes after".
- Passes the repository's gates. A change that can't yet pass them is a draft.
- Keeps its own scope: no unrelated formatting, renames or drive-by edits.
- Keeps license notices intact, and names the source and license of anything adapted from elsewhere.

## AI assistance

AI tools may be used under the terms in [AI usage](AI.md): you are the author of what you submit,
you understand it, and it passes the same gates as any other change.

## Licensing

Contributions are accepted under the license of the repository they are made to.
