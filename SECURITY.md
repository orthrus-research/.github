# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report security problems privately to admin@orthrus.cc, not in a public issue or on Discord.
Include the affected repository and version or commit, what an attacker could do, and the steps
or files that reproduce it.

Reports are acknowledged by email within 24 hours. A fix is developed privately and released
before the details are published, and the report is credited unless you ask otherwise.

## Scope

Every repository under [orthrus-research](https://github.com/orthrus-research), including
released JARs and the tools in each repository. Problems in upstream projects (Cleanroom,
CubicChunks, Distant Horizons, GregTech CE Unofficial) also belong with those projects; Orthrus
Research forwards reports that affect them.

## Supported versions

The projects are research previews without long-term support branches. Fixes land on `main` and
in the next release.
